1. Purpose

We collect personal information only to respond to your consultation request, match you with hospitals and travel partners you agree to, and confirm prices and schedules.

2. Items collected

Required: name, at least one contact method, consultation language, treatment area. Optional: nationality, country of residence, age range, budget, visit dates, travel and concierge preferences, marketing preference.

We do not collect medical records or test results through this website. If a hospital needs them, they will be collected through a secure channel managed by that hospital.

3. Sharing with third parties

Your request is shared with a hospital or travel partner only if you gave separate consent, and only the minimum information needed.

4. Retention & deletion

You can withdraw consent or request deletion at any time by contacting your coordinator. Data is de-identified after the retention period.
