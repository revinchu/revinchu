## Why shop for beauty in Korea?

Korean brands launch products quickly, price them competitively and sell them through dense networks of shops where you can test almost everything. Many items released in Korea reach overseas stores months later, if at all, and in-store promotions ("1+1", buy one get one) are common.

## Where to shop

![Myeong-dong street with beauty shops](https://tong.visitkorea.or.kr/cms/resource/96/3548996_image2_1.jpg "Korea Tourism Organization")

### Myeongdong — everything in one walk

The densest beauty district in Seoul. Health-and-beauty chain stores, brand flagships, and the Lotte and Shinsegae department stores (with their duty-free floors) are within ten minutes of each other. Staff speak English, Chinese and Japanese in many stores. It is busy in the evening; mornings are calmer.

### Seongsu-dong — concept stores and pop-ups

Converted factories now host brand experience stores and short-term pop-ups. Good for discovering new or indie brands and for photos. Check brands' social media for pop-up dates.

### Hongdae and Sinchon — trend-driven and student-priced

Younger brands, makeup and accessories at lower prices, plus street fashion. Easy to combine with cafés and evening street performances.

### Department stores and duty-free

For luxury and well-known Korean prestige brands, department store cosmetics floors and city duty-free shops offer consultations and samples. Duty-free purchases are collected at the airport, so bring your passport and flight details.

> **Tip:** Large chain stores have the same products across branches. If one is crowded, the next one is usually a few minutes' walk away.

## What to buy

| Category | Why it's worth it | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Sunscreen | Light textures, wide choice for daily wear | SPF 50+ / PA++++ labels, "no white cast" |
| Sheet masks | Inexpensive, easy gifts | Multi-packs and 1+1 promotions |
| Cleansers | Gentle low-pH options | "Weakly acidic" (약산성) labels |
| Serums and ampoules | Concentrated actives at moderate prices | Ingredients you already tolerate |
| Lip tints and cushions | Signature Korean makeup | Test shade in daylight near the entrance |

## How to shop smart

- **Patch test** new active ingredients (retinol, acids, vitamin C) before using them on your face.
- **Check dates.** Korean labels show the manufacturing date (제조일) and/or expiry (사용기한).
- **Ask for samples** — many shops include them with purchases.
- **Watch the promotions calendar.** Big chains run seasonal sales several times a year.

## Getting your tourist tax refund

Visitors staying in Korea for less than six months can get a refund of the VAT and other taxes included in eligible purchases at participating "Tax Free" shops.

1. Show your passport at checkout. Many stores deduct the tax immediately for eligible purchases.
2. If not deducted, keep the tax refund receipt.
3. At the airport, use the tax refund kiosk before or after security as instructed, and keep the goods unopened in case of inspection.

Minimum spend, per-purchase limits and total limits apply and are changed from time to time, so check the current rules at the shop or on the official tourism site.

## FAQ

### Where is the best place to buy Korean cosmetics in Seoul?

Myeongdong offers the widest choice in the smallest area, with chain stores, brand flagships and department stores. Seongsu is better for new brands and pop-ups, and Hongdae for trendy, budget-friendly makeup.

### Can foreigners get a tax refund on cosmetics in Korea?

Yes. At participating Tax Free shops, show your passport; many deduct the tax at checkout. Otherwise claim at airport kiosks with your receipt. Minimum and maximum amounts apply.

### Are Korean cosmetics cheaper in Korea?

Often, especially with in-store promotions and tax refunds, and many products are released in Korea first. Prices vary by store, so compare before buying in bulk.
