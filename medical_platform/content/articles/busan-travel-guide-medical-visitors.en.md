## Why combine treatment and travel in Busan?

Korea's second city has university hospitals, dental, eye and Korean-medicine hospitals, and a slower pace than Seoul. Beaches, markets and coastal walks give you plenty to do on light days without long journeys. Browse [hospitals in Busan](/{lang}/hospitals?region=busan) with their published prices.

## Getting there

- **From Seoul:** KTX high-speed trains take about two and a half to three hours to Busan Station.
- **By air:** Gimhae International Airport has domestic and international flights; the light rail and taxis connect to the city.
- **Around the city:** the subway covers Seomyeon, Nampo, Haeundae and Gwangalli; taxis are inexpensive for short hops.

## Where to stay

| Area | Best for | Nearby |
|---|---|---|
| Seomyeon (Busanjin-gu) | Transport hub, several hospitals and clinics | Jeonpo Café Street, shopping |
| Haeundae | Beach views, hotels, a calm base for recovery | Haeundae and Gwangalli beaches |
| Nampo / Seo-gu | Markets, university hospitals in Seo-gu | Jagalchi Market, BIFF Square, Gamcheon |

## Gentle sightseeing for recovery days

### Beaches: Haeundae and Gwangalli

Flat promenades for slow walks; Gwangalli is especially pretty at night with the lit-up Gwangan Bridge.

### Huinnyeoul Culture Village

White houses on a cliff above the sea in Yeongdo, with a coastal walking path. Some stairs, but you can choose a short section.

### Gamcheon Culture Village

Colorful hillside houses and art installations. It is steep — take a taxi or bus up and walk down, and skip it on days you need to rest.

![Gamcheon Culture Village](https://tong.visitkorea.or.kr/cms/resource/91/3365491_image2_1.jpg "Korea Tourism Organization")

### Haedong Yonggungsa and Taejongdae

A seaside temple on the rocks and dramatic coastal cliffs. Both involve some walking and steps; allow half a day each.

> **Tip:** After surgery or sedation, keep the first day free, avoid hot springs and saunas until your doctor says it's fine, and choose short walks close to your accommodation.

## What to eat in Busan

- **Dwaeji gukbap** — pork and rice soup, Busan's signature comfort food
- **Milmyeon** — chewy wheat noodles in cold broth, a Busan specialty
- **Hoe** at Jagalchi Market — choose seafood downstairs, eat upstairs (see the [market food tour](/{lang}/magazine/korean-market-food-tour))
- **Ssiat hotteok** at BIFF Square — pancakes stuffed with seeds and sugar
- **Eomuk** — Busan is known for its fish cakes

If you've had a procedure, ask your doctor before eating raw fish, spicy dishes or drinking alcohol.

## A relaxed three-day plan

1. **Day 1:** arrive, hospital consultation or check-up, early dinner of dwaeji gukbap in Seomyeon
2. **Day 2:** treatment day — rest at the hotel; evening walk on Haeundae beach if you feel well
3. **Day 3:** Huinnyeoul Culture Village and Jagalchi Market, or Gwangalli at night

## FAQ

### How do I get from Seoul to Busan?

By KTX high-speed train in about two and a half to three hours, or by domestic flight to Gimhae Airport.

### Which area of Busan is best to stay in for hospital visits?

Seomyeon is the most convenient transport hub with several hospitals nearby; Seo-gu has university hospitals near Nampo; Haeundae is best for a calm beach base.

### Is Gamcheon Culture Village suitable after treatment?

It is very hilly. Visit only when you feel well, take a taxi to the top and walk down slowly, or choose flatter places such as the beaches.
